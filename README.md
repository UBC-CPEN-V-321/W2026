# Ledger

Ledger is a native Android app for personal accounting and shared group expenses. The app opens **My Expenses**, with bottom navigation for **My Expenses**, **Group**, and **Me**. Personal expenses are available before signing in; opening Group or Me while signed out shows sign-in with a **Use without Login** button. Successful sign-in returns to My Expenses, except when continuing a group invitation. Create groups within Group; Me shows account details and logout. All screens share the existing teal/ivory Material theme.

## Features

- **Personal Account Management**. Track income and expenses offline, import bank statements or receipts, and review possible duplicates before saving. Signed-in users can manually back up and restore their ledger.
- **Expense Group Management**. Create groups, invite members, and split expenses equally, by percentage, or by custom amounts. Members can view group expenses and their individual shares.
- **Let's Go Dutch**. Split an itemized receipt by letting members claim individual items and see their shares update live. The group owner finalizes the split as a group expense.
